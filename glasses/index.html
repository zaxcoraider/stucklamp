How it works Research Privacy

See who's stuck without looking down.

Students type /stuck in your class Discord. Your G2 glasses show who has waited longest. One tap tells them you're on the way, and when five people hit the same error, one tap sends the fix to all five.

This has been tested before, on much heavier glasses

        

          
In 2018, Carnegie Mellon researchers gave teachers mixed-reality glasses that showed which students were struggling. Teachers spent their time where it mattered, and students learned more.

          
Holstein, McLaren & Aleven, Student learning benefits of a mixed-reality teacher awareness tool in AI-enhanced classrooms, AIED 2018.

        

        
Their prototype ran on a HoloLens headset, which no one wears for a whole school day. G2 looks like ordinary glasses, and your class already has Discord open.

Careful with students

          
    
            
  • Only a display name, a seat and the problem. No message history.
    • 
            
  • Nothing goes to the class until the mentor taps.
    • 
            
  • "On the way" is a private reply only that student sees.
    • 
            
  • It shows students who asked for help. It doesn't watch anyone.
